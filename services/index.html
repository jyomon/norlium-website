SERVICES

Support for home and small-business technology.

Choose the area that best describes what you need help with. If you are not sure, contact Norlium Technology and describe the problem.

Computer & Laptop Support

Troubleshooting for slow computers, startup problems, software issues, updates, storage limitations, performance problems and general system configuration.

Where appropriate, Norlium Technology can also assess whether an upgrade or repair is worthwhile before replacement is considered.

Wi-Fi & Home Technology

Help with weak Wi-Fi, router setup, connectivity problems, printers, connected devices and everyday home-network issues.

The aim is to identify the cause of the problem before recommending additional equipment or unnecessary replacement.

Small Business IT Support

Practical assistance for small organisations with workstations, business email, websites, backups, productivity tools, networking and general technology administration.

Support is designed around the size and needs of the business rather than complex enterprise systems.

Remote IT Support

Suitable software and configuration problems may be resolved remotely using a temporary customer-authorised support session.

Remote access is only used with the customer's permission and for the agreed purpose. The customer remains in control of the session.

Websites & Digital Services

Help with simple business websites, domain setup, business email, website maintenance and practical digital tools for small organisations.

Services focus on clear, maintainable solutions rather than unnecessary complexity.

Older Computer Reuse

Older computers that struggle with newer operating-system requirements may still be suitable for everyday use.

Where appropriate, Norlium Technology can assess compatible hardware and configure legitimate Linux and open-source software for browsing, office work, email and general computing.

Digital Assistance

Practical help for people who need support using online services, completing digital processes, uploading documents or navigating unfamiliar websites.

This may include GOV.UK services, council systems, education platforms and general digital-literacy support.

Technology Advice & Assessment

Independent practical guidance when you are unsure whether to repair, upgrade, replace or change your current technology setup.

Recommendations are based on what is suitable and cost-effective for the situation.

Security & Backup Basics

Help with practical cybersecurity measures such as system updates, account security, backup planning and basic device protection.

Support is intended to improve everyday security without making systems unnecessarily difficult to use.

OUR APPROACH

Understand the problem before recommending the solution.

Technology problems can have several possible causes. Norlium Technology aims to identify what is actually happening before recommending new hardware, software or additional services.

Sometimes the best solution is a simple configuration change. Sometimes an upgrade is worthwhile. Sometimes replacement is the sensible option.

Diagnose first. Explain clearly. Agree the work. Then proceed.
