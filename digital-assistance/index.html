YOU REMAIN IN CONTROL

Assistance without taking over your decisions.

Norlium can explain the digital process, help you navigate the website and assist with entering information that you provide.

You remain responsible for checking your information, understanding declarations and approving anything submitted in your name.

We help you use the service. We do not impersonate you.

IMPORTANT BOUNDARIES

Digital support is not regulated professional advice.

Norlium provides practical technology and digital-literacy assistance.

We do not provide legal, immigration, financial, medical or other regulated professional advice.

Where a decision requires specialist professional advice, you should use an appropriately qualified adviser or the relevant official service.

PRIVACY

Your information should remain yours.

Digital-assistance sessions may involve personal information, so support should be limited to what is necessary for the agreed task.

Customers should not send passwords, banking credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary website forms, email or messages.

Where possible, passwords and security codes should be entered directly by the customer.

ARABIC-LANGUAGE SUPPORT

Selected assistance may also be available in Arabic.

Norlium plans to provide selected digital-assistance services for Arabic-speaking customers, including people who may find UK online systems unfamiliar or difficult to navigate.

Arabic-language assistance will focus on explaining digital steps clearly while keeping the customer responsible for their own information and decisions.
