Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This policy explains how the Norlium Technology website uses cookies and similar technologies.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites can store on a visitor's device. They may be used for functions such as security, remembering preferences or measuring website usage.

Current use of cookies

Norlium aims to minimise the use of cookies and tracking technologies. We do not intentionally place advertising or marketing cookies through this website.

Where strictly necessary technologies are required for security, network operation or delivery of the website, they may operate without being used for advertising or behavioural profiling.

Analytics

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential tracking in the future, this policy will be updated and, where legally required, visitors will be given an appropriate choice before those technologies are used.

Third-party services

Links to external services, including email or messaging services, may take you to third-party websites or applications. Those services operate under their own privacy and cookie practices.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to inspect, delete or block cookies through their privacy settings. Blocking strictly necessary technologies may affect website functionality.
