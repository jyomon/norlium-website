SMALL BUSINESS FOCUS

Enough technology to support the business — without overcomplicating it.

Small businesses often need dependable computers, email, backups, connectivity and a professional online presence more than they need large or expensive IT systems.

Norlium Technology aims to recommend technology that is appropriate for the organisation, understandable to the people using it and practical to maintain.

Reliable systems. Clear advice. Sensible costs.

BUSINESS EMAIL & ONLINE PRESENCE

Look professional and stay organised.

A business email address and well-configured domain can make a small organisation appear more professional and easier to contact.

Norlium Technology can assist with domain configuration, business email setup, website basics and the technical settings needed to keep these services working correctly.

Where suitable, services can also be configured so the business owner retains clear control over accounts, billing and access.

BACKUP & RESILIENCE

Protect the files the business depends on.

Important business files should not exist in only one place. Hardware failure, accidental deletion or account problems can interrupt work quickly.

Norlium Technology can help review what needs protecting and recommend a simple backup approach suited to the size and needs of the organisation.

CYBERSECURITY BASICS

Reduce common risks without making work difficult.

Good security starts with practical controls such as updates, sensible account protection, backups and appropriate access to systems.

Norlium Technology provides general technology-security support and basic configuration assistance. Specialist cybersecurity, compliance, penetration testing or regulated security services are outside the scope unless separately agreed and appropriately qualified.

PRICING

Clear scope before business IT work begins.

Small jobs may be quoted directly. Larger setup, website or technical work may require an initial assessment so the requirements and scope can be understood.

Before any chargeable work begins, Norlium Technology will explain the proposed scope and agree a quotation or charging basis with the customer. Any chargeable initial assessment will also be explained and agreed in advance.

Significant additional work requires approval before it is carried out.
