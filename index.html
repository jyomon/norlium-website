REUSE BEFORE REPLACE

Before replacing your computer, explore whether an upgrade or operating system change could extend its useful life.

An older computer is not automatically an unusable computer. Norlium Technology can assess whether repair, an SSD or memory upgrade, software clean-up or a suitable Linux setup could extend its useful life.

Where appropriate, legitimate Linux and open-source software can provide a practical solution for web browsing, office work, email and everyday computing.

Upgrade when worthwhile. Replace when necessary. Reuse when practical.

Assess

Check the computer's condition, performance and suitability.

Repair

Identify practical repairs that may extend its useful life.

Upgrade

Consider storage, memory or software improvements.

Reuse or Replace

Recommend the most sensible option for your needs.
