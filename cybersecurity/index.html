HOW IT WORKS

Preventive support with your permission.

We agree what needs checking before work begins. Our initial reviews focus on non-intrusive inspection of relevant device and account security settings.

Any configuration changes require your approval. We explain the findings and provide practical recommendations for improving security.

CLEAR SERVICE BOUNDARIES

Know what is included before work begins.

These services provide basic preventive IT security reviews. They do not include penetration testing, digital forensics, formal Cyber Essentials certification, continuous monitoring or guaranteed protection against cyberattacks.

Remediation, additional software, equipment and travel charges are discussed and quoted separately where applicable.
